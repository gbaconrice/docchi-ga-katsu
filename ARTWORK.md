# Artwork

## Current release
The current UI/art refresh uses four credited third-party CC0 Duelyst character sprites (shield, reaper_mantis, archer, healer), with complete source clips and license records in `clips/`. These are not original artwork made for this game. The other twenty selectable roles retain the earlier artwork. SpritePilot CC0 effect records are in `assets/effects/`. No Behold Battle image assets or unknown-rights artwork are included. Rejected reaction images are excluded; reactions currently use text. Individual CC0 licenses do not license the whole game.

## Historical v7 original roster expansion
Six new original role atlases plus native32 slimelet, each nine poses. Art geometry/event maps/hashes are in assets/monsters/roster_units.json. Mage team cloth variants are separate PNGs; natural-color creatures use the existing team markers. That v7 increment introduced no third-party character design or additional licensed effect pack; the current four-role CC0 replacement was added later. New charge/explosion/frost/chain/burrow/split cues are original primitive canvas geometry driven by actual combat events.
